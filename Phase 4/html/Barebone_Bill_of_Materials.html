TJA1051T/3 to STM32G431 Pinout & Wiring Table

TJA1051 Pin Pin Name Connects To Voltage Function
1 TXD STM32 Pin PB9 3.3V Digital Transmit data from MCU to transceiver
2 GND STM32 Pin GND + CAN_GND 0.0V Common signal and circuit ground
3 VCC STM32 Pin 5V (USB rail) 5.0V DC Powers the high-energy differential bus driver
4 RXD STM32 Pin PB8 3.3V Digital Receive data from transceiver to MCU
5 VIO STM32 Pin 3.3V 3.3V DC CRITICAL: Sets 3.3V logic levels to protect STM32!
6 CAN_L Physical Bus CAN_L 1.5V / 2.5V Negative differential signal line
7 CAN_H Physical Bus CAN_H 2.5V / 3.5V Positive differential signal line
8 S Tie to GND 0.0V Disables silent mode; enables bidirectional CAN-FD
