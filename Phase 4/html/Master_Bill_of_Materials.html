⚠️ Critical Engineering Traps Resolved in This BOM

  • Pi 5 Under-Voltage Brownout: Stock INA226 0.1Ω resistor collapses 5V rail by 0.5V at 5A load. Fixed with 10 mΩ Dale WSL2512 (50 mV drop, leaves 5.05V, zero crash risk).
  • 5 Mbps Signal Reflection: Untwisted DC hookup wire creates 35% reflection (Γ = -0.35) and controller Bus-Off. Resolved with 100Ω Cat6 twisted pair cabling.
  • Accidental Search Traps Eliminated: ADS1115 voltage ADC rejected in favor of true INA226 power monitor; 700A roadside truck booster cable replaced with 2.54mm Prakti ribbon jumpers.
  • Oscilloscope Short Hazard: Mains-bonded probe ground clips short CAN_L to earth. Mandated two-channel math (CH1 - CH2) with clips on isolated CAN_GND.
